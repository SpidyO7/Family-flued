How To Play

  1. Face-Off. Each question opens with a face-off — the host picks whichever team buzzed in first. That team takes control of the board.
  2. Answering. The controlling team calls out answers. When the host hears a correct one, click Reveal Answer to flip it up — points are added to that team automatically.
  3. Strikes. A wrong guess is a strike. Click Add Strike to log it with a big red X. Three strikes flips control to the other team.
  4. Pass or Steal. After 3 strikes, the opposing team chooses to Pass (original team banks their points) or Steal (one guess at any remaining answer — a hit steals the whole board, a miss gives it to the original team).
  5. Scoring. Whoever has the higher score when all questions are played wins the match!
