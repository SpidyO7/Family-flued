How To Play

  1. One shared board. Both teams answer the same survey question each round.
  2. Team A goes first. Type Team A's answer and select Submit Answer. Team B then gets one turn.
  3. Flexible answer detection. The game accepts exact answers plus saved aliases, plurals, close spellings, word prefixes, and related phrases such as water bottle for Bottle.
  4. Scoring. A matched answer flips its card and awards its points to the team that found it. A card can only be claimed once.
  5. Round result. After both guesses, the remaining answers are revealed and the round winner is shown.
  6. Scoring. Whoever has the higher score when all questions are played wins the match!
